What LlamaSwap Is

LlamaSwap is a free meta-aggregator built by DefiLlama that queries several DEX aggregators at once and shows the quote with the best net output, without charging any fee of its own. Instead of picking one aggregator and hoping its route is competitive, you open the tool, enter the pair you want to trade, and let it poll providers such as 1inch, Matcha, Paraswap and others in parallel. The result is a single screen that ranks routes by what actually lands in your wallet after gas and slippage, not by whichever provider happens to have the flashiest marketing. Because the aggregator never takes custody of your tokens, the comparison step changes nothing about how the trade settles — you still approve and sign the winning route directly from your own wallet.

How LlamaSwap Finds the Best Price

Finding the best price across dozens of liquidity sources used to mean opening several aggregator tabs and comparing numbers by hand. This tool automates that comparison on a single page, and LlamaSwap keeps doing it for every trade you run.

Comparing Aggregators in One Place

When you request a quote, LlamaSwap fans the same trade out to multiple DEX aggregators simultaneously and pulls back each one's expected output. Because every aggregator already routes across dozens of pools and bridges internally, this is effectively a comparison of aggregators of aggregators — a second layer of competition that individual swap pages do not offer on their own.

No Added Fee

Most swap interfaces add a small front-end fee on top of whatever the underlying aggregator charges. This one does not; it passes along the aggregator's own quote unchanged, so the number you see is the number you get, aside from standard network gas.

Net Output, Not Headline Rate

A route that looks best on exchange rate alone can still lose if its gas cost or slippage is high. Results are sorted by net output — what you would actually receive — so a route with a slightly worse quoted rate but cheaper execution can still win the comparison.

LlamaSwap Fees and Costs

LlamaSwap itself does not charge a swap fee, but a trade still carries the costs built into the underlying route: network gas, the liquidity pools' own swap fee, and any fee the chosen aggregator applies before the quote reaches you. How to Swap With LlamaSwap, Step by Step

The flow stays the same regardless of which pair or chain you trade, which is what makes LlamaSwap quick to repeat once you've done it once.
  • Connect your wallet and select the network you want to trade on.
  • Choose the token you're selling and the token you want to receive.
  • Let the aggregator poll connected providers and review the ranked quotes.
  • Pick the route with the best net output, then check the slippage setting.
  • Approve the token if needed, then sign the swap transaction in your wallet.
  • Confirm the trade landed by checking your wallet balance or a block explorer.

Self-Custody While Using LlamaSwap

LlamaSwap never asks you to deposit into a pool it controls or hand over custody of your tokens. Every swap it routes you to is signed and broadcast from your own wallet, which keeps the security model identical to using any single aggregator directly.

What You're Actually Approving

When you confirm a swap, your wallet shows exactly which contract will spend your tokens and how much. Reading that approval before signing is the one habit that matters most; a limited approval for the exact amount you're trading is safer than an unlimited allowance left over for future trades.

The Seed Phrase Rule

No legitimate swap aggregator, including LlamaSwap, will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key. The only thing you ever sign is a transaction visible in your wallet's own interface — anything that asks for your recovery words by typing or pasting is a scam, not a feature of this tool or DefiLlama.

Chains and Tokens It Supports

LlamaSwap covers Ethereum and most major EVM-compatible networks that the aggregators it queries already support, along with the long tail of ERC-20 tokens those aggregators can route through pools and bridges. Coverage grows as DefiLlama adds new aggregator integrations, so a chain missing today can appear once an aggregator supporting it is wired in.

Checking a Token Before You Trade

Before swapping an unfamiliar token, verify its contract address independently — for an Ethereum-based token that usually means looking it up on Etherscan rather than trusting a name or logo inside any swap interface. A mismatched contract address is the most common way people end up buying a fake token that merely shares a ticker.

Comparing Routes Instead of Picking One Aggregator

Going straight to a single DEX aggregator skips a step, but it also means you only ever see that one provider's routing, even when a competitor would have filled the same trade for less. LlamaSwap exists because DefiLlama already tracks liquidity and volume across the industry and noticed that no two aggregators consistently win every trade. The best route depends on the pair, the size, and the chain being traded. Running that comparison automatically, the way this tool does, costs nothing extra and only takes the few seconds needed to poll each provider. For background on how decentralized exchanges and the liquidity pools underneath them work, ethereum.org's DeFi overview is a useful starting point. Ethereum's history as a smart-contract platform is documented on Wikipedia. DefiLlama's documentation explains how its data pipelines track the aggregators behind every quote LlamaSwap shows you.
